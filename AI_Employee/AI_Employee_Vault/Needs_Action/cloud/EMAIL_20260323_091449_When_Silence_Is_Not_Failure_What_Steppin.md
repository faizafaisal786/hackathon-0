For the past three Sundays, this newsletter was silent. Not because the ideas ended. Not because the journey stopped. But because life reminded me of something we often forget in the corporate race: Even the strongest professionals are human. Cold weather, health setbacks, and the body’s limits forced me to pause. And in that pause, I reflected deeply—not just on work, but on why we work the way we do. Corporate culture rarely celebrates pauses. Silence is mistaken for weakness. Rest is treated like disengagement. Absence is quietly penalized. But Islam and life teach us otherwise. The Corporate Lie: “If You Stop, You’ll Fall Behind” In modern organizations, momentum is everything.
